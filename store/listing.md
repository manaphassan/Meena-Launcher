## Full description (4000 max)

```
meena is a home screen built on meena ui: clean, light, open and fast. big type, open space and plain colour. no cards, no clutter. swipe sideways through five pages and see what matters at a glance.

today
- the time, the date and the hijri date
- weather with a moving icon, the day's high and low, and the next few days
- up to eight favourite tiles: regular or small, in your accent or each app's own colour. a tile can also open a shortcut, like a chat with one person
- one tile that holds four small apps
- your week from your calendar, with what is coming up next
- prayer times for your zone from jakim
- a scanner for qr codes and barcodes that keeps the scans you save
- notes with tasks you can tick off, or add to your calendar as a reminder
- screen time since midnight, with the apps you used most
- widgets from your other apps, right on today
- a nearby map with 3 km and 5 km rings, a compass, the qibla line and the sun's path
- battery: how fast it is charging, time to full and time left
- bluetooth devices you have connected, with their battery when the device shares it
- steps, distance, calories, heart rate and sleep from health connect, if you allow it
- double tap an empty spot to lock the phone, if you turn it on

inbox
- chats, email and alerts in one quiet place, grouped by app
- reply without leaving the home screen, when the app allows it
- see pictures people send, and play, pause or skip the music that's playing

news
- the feeds you choose, with pictures and a clean reader view

money
- gold, silver, bitcoin and ether over the past year on one chart, from free daily prices. search and add other coins and metals
- kijang emas coin prices from bank negara malaysia
- exchange rates in a table, with the day's change. add any currency you need
- prices from your own google sheet
- spending picked up from your bank alerts, kept on your phone

apps
- every app from a to z, with a letter index, a letter filter and search
- sort by category or by last used
- icon packs

make it yours
- your own wallpaper, dimmed so text stays easy to read
- put today's widgets in the order you like, and turn each one on or off
- dark, light and paper themes with ten accent colours

your phone, your data
- notes, saved scans, portfolio and keys are stored encrypted on your phone
- the scanner reads codes on your phone. camera pictures are never saved or sent
- no ads, no accounts, no tracking
- weather and the map get your location rounded to about 1 km, and only when those widgets are on
- every widget shows real information or tells you what it needs. nothing is made up.

meena is free, made by one person, for everyday use. tell us what feels wrong at harussani@outlook.my.
```
