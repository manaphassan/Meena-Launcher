Meena

A home screen for Android. Clean, light, open, fast.

Built on Meena UI: big type, plenty of space, flat colour and square corners. Swipe sideways through five pages:

  • today: time, weather, up to eight favourite tiles (a WhatsApp chat can be one), your week, prayer times, a QR and barcode scanner, notes with tasks, screen time, widgets from your other apps, a nearby map, battery and connected devices
  • inbox: your chats, email and alerts, grouped by app, with quick replies, pictures and the music that's playing
  • news: the feeds you choose, with a clean reader
  • money: markets, Kijang Emas, exchange rates, your own Google Sheet and spending from bank alerts
  • apps: search, a letter index and filter, and sorting by category or last used

Every widget shows real information or says what it needs. No ads, no accounts, no tracking.

Version 0.2.1 is the latest build: download it from GitHub. It is getting ready for a Google Play closed test and is not on the Play Store yet.
