Meena

A home screen for Android. Clean, light, open, fast.

Big type, plenty of space and flat colour, inspired by Windows Phone Metro. Swipe sideways through five pages:

  • today: time, weather, favourite apps, your week, prayer times, a nearby map, notes, battery and connected devices
  • inbox: your alerts and chats
  • news: the feeds you choose, with a clean reader
  • money: exchange rates, your own Google Sheet and spending from bank alerts
  • apps: search, a letter index, and sorting by category or last used

Every widget shows real information or says what it needs. No ads, no accounts, no tracking.

Version 0.1.0 is getting ready for a Google Play closed test. It is not on the Play Store yet.
