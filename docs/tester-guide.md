# Meena tester guide (v0.1.0)

Thanks for helping test Meena. It's a home screen for Android: five pages you swipe through sideways (today, inbox, news, money and apps), with big type and no clutter.

This guide covers how to join, what to try and how to tell us what you find.

## 1. Join the test

1. Send your Google account email (the one your phone's Play Store uses) to harussani@outlook.my.
2. You'll get an opt-in link. Open it on your phone, read the short note about being a tester, and opt in.
3. Install Meena from the Play Store link on that page.

Source: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9845334

> **Please stay opted in for 14 days.** Google only lets a personal developer release an app after at least 12 testers have stayed in the test for 14 days in a row. If you leave early, the count starts over. You can uninstall Meena if you need to, but please don't leave the test.
> Source: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465

## 2. Make Meena your home screen

Meena only works properly as your home app.

- **Most phones:** open Settings, search for **home app** (or go to **Apps > Default apps > Home app**), then pick **Meena**.
- **Or:** press the home button once after installing. If your phone asks which app to use, pick **Meena** and choose **Always**.

To go back to your old home screen at any time, pick it in the same setting. If you uninstall Meena, your phone goes back to its own home screen.

## 3. First run

Meena asks for a few things. Each one is optional, and anything you say no to just shows "needs setup".

| What it asks | What it's for |
|---|---|
| Location | Weather, prayer times and the nearby map. Weather gets your location rounded to about 1 km. |
| Calendar | "Your week" on today. |
| Notification access | Alerts and chats on inbox, and spending from bank alerts. The text stays on your phone. |
| Nearby devices | Connected Bluetooth devices and their battery. |
| Health Connect | Steps, distance, calories, heart rate and sleep. |

The full privacy policy is at https://manaphassan.github.io/Meena-Launcher/privacy-policy/

## 4. What to try

Spend a few minutes on each page in the first days, then just use your phone as normal.

**Today**
- Does the weather match your area? Tap it for the detail page.
- Add favourites: on the apps page, long-press an app and choose **add to favourites**. Try the tile that holds four small apps.
- Are the prayer times right for your zone?
- Open the nearby map. Do the 3 km and 5 km rings, the compass and the qibla line look right?
- Write a note, edit it, make it a list and set a reminder.
- Watch the battery line while charging and unplugged. Do the times seem sensible?
- Connect Bluetooth earphones. Do they show up, with their battery?

**Inbox**
- Do new alerts and chats appear? Can you open and clear them?

**News**
- Add a feed you read. Do the pictures load? Is the reader view easy to read?

**Money**
- Do the exchange rates load? If you use Google Sheets, try linking a sheet.
- If your bank sends alerts, does spending get picked up correctly? Does it ever pick up something that isn't spending?

**Apps**
- Search for an app. Use the letter index on the right.
- Switch between **a to z**, **by category** and **last used**.
- Install and uninstall an app. Does the list update?

**Everywhere**
- Anything slow, stuck, cut off, hard to read or hard to tap?
- Anything that shows made-up looking numbers? Meena should only ever show real information, or say what it needs.
- Any words that feel confusing?

## 5. Tell us what you find

- **In the Play Store:** open Meena's page and send private feedback from there (testers can do this during the test), or
- **By email:** harussani@outlook.my

Helpful things to include:
- What you did, what you expected and what happened instead.
- Your phone model and Android version (Settings > About phone).
- A screenshot, if it helps. Please crop out anything personal: messages, names, balances or your location.

If Meena crashes or freezes, a rough time it happened helps a lot.

## 6. Leaving the test

When the 14 days are over, you're welcome to keep using Meena. If you'd rather stop, set your old home app back (section 2) and uninstall it. Thank you!
