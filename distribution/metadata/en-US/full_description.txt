meena is a free, personal-use android launcher that puts what matters first.

built on meena ui, its own design language, meena shows your day at a glance: weather, calendar, chats, email, health, finance, notes and your favourite apps — all on one scrollable today page.

what makes meena different:

• meena ui — a design language built on four words: clean, light, open, fast. no cards, no rounded corners, no clutter. just text, space and colour.

• five pages — today, inbox, news, money, apps. swipe between them. each one does its job.

• favourite tiles — pin up to 8 apps as square meena tiles. colourful or monochrome, small or regular. add whatsapp contact shortcuts for one-tap chats.

• live tiles — tiles flip to show unread counts, the next calendar event or alarm, without opening the app.

• inbox — chats, email and alerts from every app, grouped into threads. music player controls when something is playing.

• notes — quick notes with bullet journal markers: dashes for notes, stars for tasks, x for done. tap to toggle.

• scanner — built-in qr and barcode scanner with history. no extra app needed.

• colourful tiles — each app's tile uses its own dominant colour. or keep the classic monochrome meena look.

• screen time — see how long you've used each app today.

• privacy first — no account, no cloud, no tracking. api keys and personal data are encrypted on your phone with android keystore. nothing leaves the device.

• open and fast — r8-optimised, no background services beyond the notification listener you choose to enable.

meena is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases. made by one person, for everyday use.

permissions explained:
- camera: qr/barcode scanner
- location: weather and nearby map
- calendar: agenda widget
- notification access: inbox, spending tracker, live tiles
- bluetooth: connected devices widget
- health connect: steps, sleep, heart rate
